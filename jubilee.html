2. The 7 Strategic Education Pillars

Pillar 01 The Land Inflation Crisis

Over the last decade, land inflation accounts for 35%–60% of home list prices. Buyers are not priced out by the house; they are priced out by the dirt beneath it.

Pillar 02 Two-Step Mechanics

Step 1: Purchase and finance the physical home structure with a standard mortgage. Step 2: Acquire the land over time when personal capital allows.

Pillar 03 Institutional Trust & Deeds

The buyer holds a 100% recorded title deed in public county records, financed through regulated conventional mortgage channels.

Pillar 04 100% Structure Equity

Every dollar of mortgage principal paid down and 100% of structure price appreciation belongs exclusively to the homeowner. Jubilee takes 0%.

Pillar 05 Autonomy & Inheritance

Full freedom to remodel, add ADUs, install pools, and house pets. The home and recorded ground agreement are fully inheritable real property assets.

Pillar 06 Balance vs. Rate Protection

Borrowing 40%–60% less principal delivers 3x–4x the payment relief of an elusive 1% Federal Reserve rate cut immediately at today's rates.

Pillar 07 The Transparent Land Buyback Pathway

Homeowners have the recorded right to purchase the land at any time based on objective third-party property datasets (AEI Housing Center) with zero prepayment penalties or forced buyout dates.
