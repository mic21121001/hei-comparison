1. The Big Idea: Simple & Clear

Over the last ten years, land prices exploded. Today, when you buy a house, half the price tag isn't the building—it's the expensive dirt underneath it. Traditional banks make you borrow money for both, driving up your down payment and monthly mortgage.

The Two-Step Fix

Two-Step Homeownership separates the house from the land. You buy and own the actual home today with a normal mortgage. Jubilee covers the land. That means a much smaller loan, a lower down payment, and a monthly payment that actually fits your budget. You can buy the land whenever you're ready—or just enjoy owning your home.

2. The 7 Things Every Buyer Needs to Know

01 The Land Problem

You aren't priced out by the house. You're priced out by the land. We take the land cost out of the equation so you can afford the home.

02 The 2 Steps

Step 1: Buy the house today with a standard home loan.
Step 2: Buy the land down the road whenever you're ready.

03 Your Name on the Deed

You aren't a renter. Your name goes on the official county title deed at closing just like any normal home purchase.

04 You Keep Your Equity

Every dollar of home value growth and every mortgage payment you make builds equity that belongs 100% to you. Jubilee takes zero percent.

05 Total Homeowner Freedom

Paint the walls, remodel the kitchen, add a pool, or get pets. You have complete control, and you can leave the home to your children.

06 Don't Wait on Rates

Borrowing $400k at today's rates gives you a much lower payment than borrowing $800k at lower rates later. Smaller loans win every time.

07 Clear Land Buyback

Buy the land in year 1, year 5, or year 15. The price is set by trusted, public housing market data—no hidden fees, no balloon payments, and no deadlines.

3. The 3 Buyer Types & How We Talk to Them

1. Families Needing Space (Buy Boosters) Affordability

Who they are: Families squeezed in cramped rentals or condos who need a real backyard and good schools.

What to say: "Get the single-family home and school district your kids need today—without maxing out your budget."

The Math: Buy an $800k house on a $500k budget with up to 75% less cash upfront.

2. Smart Money Savers (Capital Optimizers) Liquidity

Who they are: High earners and professionals who have money, but hate the idea of locking $150k in cash into dirt.

What to say: "Own the home and all its upside while keeping six figures of cash growing in your investments."

The Math: Put down a fraction of the cash, keep $100k+ in the stock market, and build home equity at the same time.

3. "Not Ready... Yet" (Turnaround Path) Credit & Savings

Who they are: People who got turned down by a traditional bank because of credit scores or debt.

What to say: "A 'not yet' is not a 'no.' Because you only finance the home, the bar to qualify is much lower. Here is your step-by-step fix."

The Math: You only need to save $10k–$15k instead of $50k. We give you a 6-to-12 month plan to get approved.

Block 05: Frequently Asked Questions Direct Answers
Q: Do I actually own the home?
A: Yes. Your name is on the legal deed recorded with the county. You have the exact same rights as any homeowner to remodel, rent it out, refinance, or sell it.
Q: What happens when I want to sell?
A: You list it on the MLS with an agent just like any house. You keep 100% of your equity and renovation gains at closing.
Q: What if I never buy the land?
A: That's totally fine. There is no balloon payment, no forced deadline, and no rush. Your agreement is secured for up to 99 years.
Q: Can I leave the house to my kids?
A: Yes. Both the home deed and the land agreement can be passed down to your children or heirs in your will.
