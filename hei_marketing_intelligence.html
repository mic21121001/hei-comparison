💡 Key Insights — Week of July 23–29, 2026
🚨 Splitero Meta Surge — 364% in One Week
Splitero went from ~28 to ~130 active Meta ads in a single week — a 364% increase with new ads launched July 23. Combined with their NFL/NHL/Disney partnerships and Fiat Growth's structure, this signals a deliberate acceleration of acquisition marketing. The most important competitive development this week.
✅ Unlock Scaling on Meta
Unlock's Meta presence grew +50 ads week over week (160 → 210) while holding steady on Google at ~500. Combined digital footprint now ~710 — strongest pure-play HEA total. New "Reverse Mortgage Alternative" messaging expands the comparison intercept strategy to a new audience segment.
⚠ Point's Meta Pullback
Point dropped 110 Meta ads week over week (250 → 140) — their biggest single-week decline. Google edged up slightly (+5). This is likely a creative refresh cycle before a new campaign push, but worth monitoring. If they don't rebound next week it signals a strategic channel shift.
📈 Hometap Expanding Audience Targeting
Hometap added new use-case extensions on Google — "Fund Your Growing Family," "Fund Your Small Business," "Fund Your Retirement" — broadening beyond HELOC comparison to specific life moments. Meta also up +68. They're the second-biggest mover this week after Splitero. New brand framing: "Smart New Loan Alternative."
⚠ Stale Unlock Creative Still Running
"15,000 homeowners" still active in Unlock's Google creative rotation — should be 20,000+. Also inconsistent star ratings (4.7★ vs 4.8★) across creatives. Underselling social proof by 25%. Needs a creative audit — older variants are still being served by the algorithm.
🔍 SIGE (USA) on Splitero — Watch
An unusual third-party advertiser — SIGE (USA) Information Technology Co., Ltd — appeared running ads pointing to splitero.com in the Google Ads Transparency results. Could be an affiliate, a grey-market reseller, or an anomaly. Worth tracking next week to see if it persists or disappears.
