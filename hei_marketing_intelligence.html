💡 Key Insights — Week of July 22, 2026
🥇 Unlock Owns Google Search
With ~500 Google ads in 7 days, Unlock runs nearly 7x more Google ads than Point and 1.7x more than Hometap. Unlock is capturing homeowners at the moment of active search intent — the highest-value acquisition moment in the funnel.
📱 Point Owns Meta Social
Point runs ~250 active Meta ads vs Unlock's 160 — building awareness upstream of search intent. Their emotional/video creative ("peace of mind", "your home can send cash your way") is more sophisticated than any competitor on social.
⚠️ Hometap Has Most Social Proof
Hometap leads on reviews with 4.9★ from 2,071 verified reviews — more than any competitor. They're actively showing this in ads. Unlock shows 4.8★ from 1,392. Review count is a meaningful trust signal at this stage of consumer education.
📈 Point Is Dominant on Volume
Point's $508.6M July 2026 securitization is the largest HEI deal in history. Their H1 2026 securitized volume of $850M+ significantly outpaces all competitors. Point's 2025 growth was 3x year-over-year per their own press releases.
🆕 Nada Just Launched Meta
Nada launched 19 new Meta ads on July 21 — one day ago. This is a signal of increased marketing investment worth monitoring. Their "transparent pricing" and retirement-focused messaging is differentiated. Watch for Google ramp-up in coming weeks.
🔍 JGW Not a Real Paid Media Competitor
Despite ~600 Google ads and ~81 Meta ads, JGW's entire paid media presence is debt relief and structured settlements — not HEA. Their HEA product has essentially no paid media footprint at this time. Not a meaningful digital marketing competitor.
