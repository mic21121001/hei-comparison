Introduction

Unlock is an innovative consumer finance company on a mission to help homeowners unlock the value of their biggest asset, their home equity, and to dramatically improve their lives by doing so.

The company was founded by respected real estate and finance veterans with deep experience in innovative consumer finance products. We offer an innovative home finance solution, called a Home Equity Agreement (HEA).

What's The Catch?

We get this question all the time. People wonder how we can offer homeowners money for up to 10 years with no monthly payments. Where does the money come from?

Put simply, there is no catch. The HEA is a new type of home finance that differs from traditional mortgage loans and home equity lines of credit. Those products require homeowners to make monthly payments based on a specified interest rate. Investors in those products find earning interest attractive. HEAs allow homeowners to defer the cost of the financing until settlement and then share a percentage of the home's value at that time. HEA investors find that attractive.

Knowledge Checklist

Education is a key part of who we are as a company and it is a required step in our process. Before closing your transaction, please review the following foundational concepts:

  • HEA Basics: We establish a Starting Home Value via appraisal. You receive a lump sum upfront. You make no monthly payments and settle up anytime within a 10-year term based on the Ending Home Value.
  • The Multiplier: A multiplier determines Unlock's percentage at the end (typically 1.8x). For example, if you receive 10% of the Starting Home Value up front, Unlock receives 18% of the Ending Home Value.
  • Cost Limits: The gross cost is capped by the Annualized Cost Limit, typically 19.9%.
  • Property Use: Pricing varies between principal residences, second homes, and investment properties due to risk factors. Converting property use during the term triggers pricing adjustments.
  • Improvement Adjustments: If you make significant home improvements that increase your home's value, you can request an adjustment to lower the Ending Home Value so Unlock doesn't share in the added value you paid for.

The cost of your Unlock HEA is unknown up front because it depends on changes in your home's value and the length of time you keep your HEA. Key financial concepts include:

Homeowner Responsibilities

During the 10-year term, you must stay current on housing obligations (mortgage, property taxes, insurance), maintain the home in proper working order (subject to normal wear and tear), and inform Unlock of any major property or financial changes.

Maintenance Adjustment

If you fail to maintain your home in proper working order, and your home's value is significantly lower at the time of settlement as a result, Unlock may make a Maintenance Adjustment which increases the Ending Home Value for calculation purposes so Unlock does not share in a loss caused by improper maintenance.
