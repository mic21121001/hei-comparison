Unlock Channel Gaps Channels competitors are actively running that Unlock is not — ranked by strategic relevance to HEI acquisition
LinkedIn Ads

Hometap is the only HEI player running LinkedIn — likely targeting homeowners in finance, tech, and professional roles, or reaching mortgage brokers and advisors. Given Unlock's focus on partnerships, LinkedIn is a natural fit for broker/partner outreach.

Running: Hometap only

Dedicated A/B Testing (VWO / Optimizely)

Hometap runs VWO for landing page and funnel optimization. Split.io (which Unlock uses) is an engineering feature-flag tool, not a marketer-friendly CRO platform. A dedicated A/B testing tool would allow faster experimentation on landing pages, CTAs, and application flows without engineering involvement.

Running: Hometap (VWO)

Anonymous Visitor Retargeting (BouncePilot)

BouncePilot identifies anonymous site visitors — people who looked at the site but didn't fill out a form — and enables email outreach to them within hours. For a high-consideration product like HEI where people research for weeks, this could meaningfully recover lost pipeline. Only Hometap is running it.

Running: Hometap only

Spotify Ads

Hometap is running Spotify across both Ad Studio and the Audience Network. Homeowners are heavy podcast and playlist listeners — home improvement, personal finance, and real estate podcasts are natural environments. Unlock already has Audacy/AdsWizz for podcast buying via TTD but Spotify's first-party audience data is distinct.

Running: Hometap only

Session Recording (Contentsquare)

Hometap uses Contentsquare to understand where visitors drop off in the application funnel. Without session recording, identifying friction in the UX requires guesswork. Given the complexity of the HEI application, understanding where people hesitate or abandon could unlock significant conversion rate improvement.

Running: Hometap only

Nextdoor Ads

Splitero is running Nextdoor — a hyperlocal platform where homeowners in specific neighborhoods discuss home topics. Lower volume than Meta or Google but potentially very high intent. Worth testing given Unlock's local market strategy.

Running: Splitero only
